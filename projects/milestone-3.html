AES-CBC Decryption via CyberChef

Decrypt an AES-256-CBC ciphertext by getting the key, IV, mode, and encoding right. I diagnosed a partial decryption caused by a wrong IV and corrected it to recover the message.

# Approach

I first tried to crack this with OpenSSL and it did not go my way, so after exhausting those attempts I switched to CyberChef, where it was relatively straightforward. I chose AES Decrypt, loaded the ciphertext file, and supplied the provided key file in UTF8.

My first IV produced only a partial, garbled decryption. I diagnosed that the IV was the problem, corrected it, and with the right mode and encoding the message decrypted cleanly.
